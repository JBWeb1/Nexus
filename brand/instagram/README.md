# Instagram posters

Four portrait posts at 1080 × 1350 (Instagram's tallest feed size), made with real screens from the app and the Nexus logo.

| File | Message |
|---|---|
| `nexus-ig-1-get-paid.png` | "Still chasing clients to pay you?": reminders by email or WhatsApp, customers pay online |
| `nexus-ig-2-month-end.png` | "Month end, sorted.": receipts from a photo, bank matching, VAT201, reports to Excel |
| `nexus-ig-3-books-check-themselves.png` | "Books that check themselves.": the books-health checks, locked entries, activity log |
| `nexus-ig-4-r250-everything-included.png` | "R250 a month. Everything included.": the features, 14 days free, no card needed |

Remake them after a change with `node brand/make-instagram-posters.mjs <repo root> brand/instagram` (needs playwright-core and Chromium).

## "Picture it" set

| File | Message |
