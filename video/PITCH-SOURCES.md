# Pitch video: where each number comes from

| On screen | Source |
|---|---|
| 790,000 formal small businesses in South Africa | SEDA estimate of formalised SMMEs, as reported in mid-2024 (out of about 2.7 million SMMEs in total). TIPS put formal small businesses at almost 700,000 for 2024 using a different definition. Estimates vary widely. |
| Xero South Africa: R450 / R795 / R1,095 a month, Starter limited to 20 invoices | Xero ZA list prices, checked October 2026. Re-check before each use: prices change. |
| R250 a month per company, everything included | Nexus Ledger's own price. |
| 1% = 7,900 businesses = about R23.7m a year | Arithmetic only: 790,000 × 1% × R250 × 12. Shown on screen as an illustration, not a forecast. |
| 1 firm with 8 clients = R2,000 a month | Arithmetic only: 8 × R250. |

The video makes no claim about customers, users or revenue to date. Add a contact line by setting `CONTACT` in `src/Pitch.tsx` and re-rendering.
