Why we built it

Running a business is hard enough. Your books should not be. Nexus works out the VAT, matches the bank, chases late payers and tells you when something does not add up, so you can get back to the work that pays.

01 · INVOICE

Send a proper tax invoice in seconds.

Pick the customer and the product. Nexus fills in the price, works out the 15% VAT and sends a PDF that SARS would be happy to see.

02 · GET PAID

Customers pay with one tap.

Every invoice has a pay link. Friendly reminders go out on email or WhatsApp when payment is late, so you never have to chase.

03 · BANK

The bank matches itself.

Import your statement and Nexus pairs each line with the invoice or bill it belongs to. Rules handle the rest, like bank fees and rent.

04 · VAT201

VAT201 ready before you are.

Output VAT, input VAT and what you owe, worked out from the books and locked once you file. No spreadsheet, no surprises.
